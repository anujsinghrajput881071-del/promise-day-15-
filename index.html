Promise Day 🤞

Aaj ka din thoda khaas lag raha hai.

Shayad isliye kyunki tumhari yaad kuch zyada hi aa rahi hai.

Bina kisi wajah ke bhi, tumhara khayal sukoon deta hai.

Kuch rishte bol kar nahi, mehsoos karke samjhe jaate hain.

Aur ye baat main dil se keh pa raha hoon.

In English we say:
Promise 🤞

But in shayari 📜
Aladin said:
Yun to waadon ka shor bahut hota hai duniya mein,
Magar jo tumse kiya, usey jeena jaanta hoon,
Haath chhodne ki baat kabhi dil mein aayi hi nahi,
Saath chalna hi nahi, saath rehna maanta hoon.

Fir bhi agar tumko koi promise chahiye, to be-jijhak bolo.

— Tumhara Bhola Bhala Future Husband 🩷
